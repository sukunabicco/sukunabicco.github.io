Jishin Sensei ("Earthquake Teacher") is an iOS app that turns Japan's earthquake wisdom into a fun daily habit. Every day, Namazu Sensei — the legendary catfish of Japanese earthquake folklore — asks you one real-life scenario question.

FAQ

Is the app free?
Yes — all of it. The full 68-question deck, Study Mode, Challenge, the Prepare tab and the Emergency tab are free for everyone (version 1.0.6). If you like the sensei, you can leave an optional tip in Settings; it unlocks nothing.
I bought the Supporter unlock in an earlier version. What happens now?
Nothing is taken away: everything it unlocked is now free for everyone, and your supporter badge stays. To show it on a new device, sign in with the same Apple Account and open Settings → Support the sensei → Restore purchases.
Does the Emergency tab work offline?
Yes. Emergency numbers and guides are bundled with the app and work without any connection.
Where do the answers come from?
Every question cites official English sources from Japanese public authorities, such as the Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) and the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO).
Is my data collected?
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. See the privacy policy for details.

Emergency disclaimer

The app's safety content is educational. In an actual emergency, always follow instructions from local authorities.
