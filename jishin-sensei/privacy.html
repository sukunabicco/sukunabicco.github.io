Privacy Policy — Jishin Sensei

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Jishin Sensei ("the app") is developed and operated by Makoto Katano.

The short version

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and collects no personal information. We don't want your data.

The website's contact form is separate from the app: it receives only what you type into it. See Contact form below.

What stays on your device

Your quiz answers, streak, and preferences are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to any server, and deleting the app deletes them.

Your health card and trip card are kept in the iPhone's Keychain. They are never sent to the developer or to any server, and they are not synced with iCloud Keychain. Like other Keychain items, they can be included in an encrypted backup of your iPhone and restored to a new iPhone from that backup. iOS keeps the Keychain even after an app is deleted, so if you reinstall the app on the same iPhone, the cards come back. To erase them, empty each entry in the card inside the app before deleting it — changes are saved as you make them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To deliver purchases and restore them across reinstalls, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier and App Store purchase receipts. It does not receive your name, email address, Apple Account, or any contact information from us.

  • RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

External links

The app links to official public-safety resources (such as the Japan Meteorological Agency, the Japan National Tourism Organization, and mobile carriers' alert-sound demonstration pages). When you open those links, the respective sites' own terms and privacy practices apply.

Emergency information disclaimer

The app's safety content is educational and based on official public sources. In an actual emergency, always follow instructions from local authorities.

Contact form (website)

The website has a contact form, run on Google Forms. It is separate from the app — the app itself still collects no personal information.

  • What we receive: what you type into the form — the topic, your email address and your message, and, if you choose to give them, your name (you may stay anonymous), your requests, your property or company name, and how you heard about the app.
  • What we use it for: only to reply to you and to improve the app. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not share or sell it.
  • Where it is kept: in Google Forms and Google Sheets under the developer's Google account. Google handles it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How long: deleted within 12 months after our last exchange, or sooner if you ask.
  • Deletion: reply to any email from us, or ask through the form.
