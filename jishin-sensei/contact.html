English

Use the form below for anything about the app — press, feedback, or questions about displaying it. The printable QR poster for hotels (A4, in English, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese) is a free download from the press kit. No phone number needed.

What you send is used only to reply to you and to improve the app, and it is deleted within 12 months of our last exchange. See the privacy policy. The app itself collects no personal information.
